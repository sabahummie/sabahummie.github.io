## Short Bio

I am Sabah Ummie, an AI/ML healthcare researcher at [Elite Research Lab](https://elitelab.ai/). I earned my M.S. from the [Electrical, Computer, and Biomedical Engineering (ECBE)](https://web.uri.edu/ece/) Department at the University of Rhode Island, where I had the privilege of completing my thesis at the [Wearable Biosensing Lab (WBL)](https://sites.google.com/uri.edu/wbl) under the supervision of [Dr. Kunal Mankodiya](https://www.ele.uri.edu/faculty/kunalm/).

## Research Interest

My research interests lie at the intersection of applied machine learning and the reliability of AI in healthcare and biomedical sciences. I am deeply focused on the explainability of AI and its ability to generate scientifically backed outcomes for early disease prediction. To achieve this, my work frequently involves analyzing data starting from patient demography to high-dimensional genomic expression datasets. I am also passionate to explore the interpretability of advanced LLM agents to ensure clinical tools are both powerful and trustworthy.

I am actively seeking Ph.D. positions for Fall 2027 in areas including, but not limited to, AI Explainability and Interpretability, Human-AI Interaction, and Responsible AI. I am eager to bring my experience in developing end-to-end, reproducible machine learning workflows to research group dedicated to advancing trustworthy AI in the health and biomedical sciences.
