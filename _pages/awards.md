### Blue Skies Innovations to Improve QOL
**Epilepsy Foundation of New England** · *2022*
**Grant Amount:** \$75,000
**Funded Project:** *RISE ABOVE: Web-Based Education Portal for People with Epilepsy*

Secured funding to develop and evaluate a patient-centered, web-based intervention designed to reduce internalized stigma and improve social well-being among people living with epilepsy.
